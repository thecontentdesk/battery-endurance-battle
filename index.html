Battery Capacity Sets the Foundation

Battery capacity gives an idea of how much energy a device can store. Manufacturers usually measure it in milliampere-hours (mAh). A higher mAh rating generally means more stored energy. However, it does not guarantee longer operating time.

A device with a powerful coil may consume energy faster than one with a more efficient heating system. This is why two devices with similar battery ratings can deliver different results.

Understanding Real Battery Performance

Battery endurance depends on how efficiently a device uses stored energy. Frequent activation, long draws, and high-power settings can increase consumption. Meanwhile, moderate usage can help reduce unnecessary battery drain.

The Role of Coil Technology

Mesh coils provide a larger heating surface. They can distribute heat more evenly and support consistent vapour production. However, their energy consumption still depends on resistance, power output, and device design.
